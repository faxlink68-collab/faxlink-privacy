FaxLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

FaxLink provides mobile fax services that allow users to prepare, send and manage fax documents. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and shared when you use FaxLink.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Account and device information: Information used to identify your app installation and provide the service.
  • Fax information: Fax recipient numbers, documents and related delivery information that you submit.
  • Contacts: Contact details only when you choose to grant contact access or select contacts within the app.
  • Purchase information: Subscription or purchase transaction details needed to verify purchases and provide access.
  • Technical information: Information about app operation, errors and device compatibility where available.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate FaxLink services.
  • Transmit documents to the fax numbers you select.
  • Display fax history and delivery status.
  • Verify purchases and manage access to paid features.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support requests.

3. Fax Documents and Third-Party Providers

When you send a fax, the document and recipient information must be processed by the service providers involved in fax transmission. FaxLink uses third-party infrastructure and fax service providers to operate its services. Those providers may process information as necessary to deliver the requested fax.

4. Payments

Purchases made through Apple's App Store are processed by Apple. FaxLink does not receive or store your complete payment card details. Transaction information may be used to verify purchases and manage access to paid features.

5. Permissions

FaxLink may request access to device features such as contacts, camera or photo library when needed for a feature you choose to use. You can manage app permissions through your device settings.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate FaxLink, including fax transmission, infrastructure and payment verification providers, or when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

7. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, legitimate business purposes, legal obligations and dispute resolution.

8. Your Choices

You can control permissions such as contacts, camera and photo access through your device settings. You may contact us with questions or requests concerning your personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

FaxLink is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
